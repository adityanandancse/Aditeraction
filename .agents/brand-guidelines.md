## PART 1 — BRAND FOUNDATION

### About This Guide

This is the single source of truth for how Aditeraction looks, sounds and behaves. It's written so any designer, copywriter, marketer or collaborator — in-house or external — can produce on-brand work without asking for clarification. Use Part 1 to understand what the brand stands for, Parts 2–4 for the rules of execution (visual, verbal, messaging), and Part 5 for applying them to real touchpoints. Where an item is marked **PROVISIONAL**, treat it as the current recommended direction and confirm with the brand guardian (Part 6) before final production.

### Brand Essence

**Mission**: Aditeraction exists to help founders anywhere launch brands, sites and apps that look as good as their idea — by putting design and engineering under one roof and owning the journey from first sketch to live product.

**Vision**: To be the studio founders trust to take an idea all the way to launch — proof that global-grade craft doesn't require an agency's overheads, timelines or layers.

**Values**:
- **Own the whole outcome** — We're accountable for the launch, not just the deliverable.
- **Craft you can feel** — Every pixel, line of code and frame is intentional; no templates, no shortcuts that show.
- **Design and code are one discipline** — We design what we can build and build what we design, so nothing is lost in handoff.
- **Say it straight** — Honest timelines, honest pricing, the hard thing said early.
- **Show, don't claim** — Credibility comes from shipped work, never borrowed stats or empty promises.

**Brand Personality**: Bold · Precise · Playful · Straight-talking · Crafted

**Positioning Statement**: For founders launching or relaunching a brand, product or app without an in-house design or tech team, Aditeraction is the one partner that designs the brand and ships the production code under one roof — delivering agency-level craft at a lean studio's speed and cost, across Indian and international time zones.

## PART 2 — VISUAL IDENTITY

> **LOCKED:** The accent colour, neutrals and typefaces below are final and approved. (The tagline in Part 4 remains provisional.)

### Logo

**Primary logo** — A **symbol + wordmark** lockup. The symbol is the **"code nib" mark**: code brackets `<` `>` framing a solid Vermilion pen nib — the brackets are engineering, the nib is design. It reads as a distinctive constructed glyph, *not* a letter. The wordmark sets **"aditeraction" in Space Grotesk SemiBold, outlined, tracking −0.035em, with both i-dots rendered as Vermilion squares**. The lockup is the default for most applications; master files live in `D:\Aditya\Aditeraction\brand\`.

**Logo variations** (all to be produced and proofed before launch):
- **Horizontal lockup** — symbol + wordmark side by side (primary).
- **Stacked lockup** — symbol above wordmark, for square/narrow spaces.
- **Icon-only** — the standalone code-nib mark (favicon, app icon, social avatar).
- **Reversed** — light-on-dark (Paper on Ink) and dark-on-light (Ink on Paper).
- **One-colour** — solid single-colour version for stamps, embossing, single-colour print.

**Clear space rule** — Minimum clear space on all sides equals the height of the nib within the symbol. Nothing — text, image edge, other logos — enters this zone.

**Minimum size** —
- Icon-only: legible and tested at **16px** (favicon). Never smaller.
- Full lockup: **24px** tall on screen / **18mm** wide in print. Below this, switch to the icon-only mark.

**Incorrect usage** — never:
- Stretch, distort, rotate or re-proportion the mark or wordmark.
- Add drop shadows, glows, bevels, or any 3D effect to the logo itself (cinematic/3D treatment lives in the hero environment, never on the mark).
- Apply gradients to the logo.
- Place it on a busy background without the Ink/Paper container or sufficient contrast.
- Recolour it outside the approved palette (no vermilion-on-vermilion; no unapproved hues).
- Outline the logo, add strokes, or reconstruct the wordmark by typing it in a different font.
- Rebuild the symbol as a generic "A" monogram or add clichés (rockets, swooshes, etc.).

**Color usage rules:**
- **Default experience is dark**: Ink canvas, Paper text, Vermilion accent. Light mode uses Paper canvas with Ink text.
- **One accent moment per screen** — Vermilion earns attention by being rare (a link, active state, the mark's node, one key word).
- **Approved combinations**: Paper on Ink; Vermilion on Ink; Ink on Paper; Graphite on Ink or Paper (secondary text); Fog cards on Ink.
- **Prohibited**: Vermilion text for long body copy on Paper (fails contrast); vermilion on vermilion; gradients between accent and neutral; glows/neon bloom; vermilion as a default background (allowed only as a deliberate full-bleed statement frame).
- **Accessibility (digital)**: body text must meet WCAG AA — **≥4.5:1** contrast (≥3:1 for large text ≥24px/19px-bold). Paper (#F4F1EC) on Ink (#0B0B0D) passes comfortably. Vermilion (#FF4D2E) on Ink passes for large/bold and UI accents but **must not** be used for small body text; Graphite is for secondary text only, never fine print below AA.

### Typography

> **LOCKED.** Two grotesques + a monospace, all free: Space Grotesk (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (labels/meta/code).

**Primary typefaces** — *Space Grotesk* (display / headlines) — distinct, slightly playful geometry — paired with *Inter* (body) — neutral, screen-optimised, huge weight range. Weights in use: Regular, Medium, SemiBold/Bold (display); Regular, Medium (body).

**Secondary typeface** — *JetBrains Mono* — the engineering "signal". Weights: Regular, Medium. Used for labels, metadata, section numbers (`01 —`), case-study specs, tags, timestamps, code and small UI detail.

**Type hierarchy**

| Level | Typeface | Weight | Size range | Usage |
|-------|----------|--------|------------|-------|
| H1 | Display grotesque | Bold | 48–96px | Hero headlines, declarative statements |
| H2 | Display grotesque | Medium/Bold | 32–48px | Section headlines |
| H3 | Display grotesque | Medium | 22–30px | Subheads |
| Body | Body grotesque (Inter) | Regular | 16–18px | Paragraph copy |
| Caption | Body grotesque (Inter) | Regular | 13–14px | Image captions, footnotes (Graphite) |
| Label | Monospace | Medium | 11–13px | Meta, specs, section numbers, tags (often letter-spaced) |

**Typography rules:**
- **Line height**: headlines 1.0–1.1 (tight); body 1.5–1.65 (generous, for dark-mode legibility); labels 1.2.
- **Letter spacing**: headlines slightly tight (−1% to −2%); mono labels slightly open (+4% to +8%), often uppercase; body default.
- **Headlines are short and declarative** (matches "say it straight"); avoid long multi-line H1s.
- **Fallback stack** when brand fonts aren't available:
  - Display/body: `"Space Grotesk", Inter, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`
  - Mono: `"JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, monospace`
- **Avoid**: serifs/slabs, humanist/rounded "friendly" fonts, novelty display faces, scripts/handwritten, condensed "sports" grotesques, and system Arial/Helvetica as a *brand* face (fallback only).

### Imagery

**Photography style** — Cinematic and dark, high-contrast, editorial. The mood of a studio at work after hours — focused, premium, real. Imagery is *evidence of craft*, not stock mood. Subjects: the work on real devices; process (design artboards beside code editors in one frame); people sparingly and candid; restrained WebGL/3D renders derived from the mark's grid, for the hero only. Colour treatment: deep shadows, controlled highlights, largely desaturated — with one deliberate vermilion element in frame.

**Illustration style** — Minimal and structural: grids, nodes, vector paths, wireframe-to-solid transitions drawn from the mark. No characters, mascots or decorative spot illustration. Motion-first where possible. Single-weight line, Ink/Paper/Vermilion only.

**Iconography** — Monoline line icons on a strict grid; sharp or minimally-rounded corners; single consistent stroke (~1.5–2px optical at base). Primarily functional; one or two branded icons may carry the interaction-node detail. No filled/duotone mixing.

**What to avoid** — Agency stock clichés (handshakes, teams-around-a-laptop, staged sticky-note walls); over-produced glossy renders; AI-gradient/iridescent blobs; lens flares and bokeh; bright flat-lay "startup" imagery; template heroes; region-specific visual shorthand for "India" or "global".

## PART 3 — VERBAL IDENTITY

### Brand Voice

**Voice Essence** — Aditeraction sounds like a sharp, senior maker who tells you the truth and shows you the work — casual but exact, confident without the jargon.

**Tone dimensions**
- **Straight** — Plain, honest, specific. Short sentences. Says the hard thing early.
- **Sharp** — Precise word choice; every line earns its place. Confident, never hedging.
- **Playful** — Dry wit and a light touch in how ideas are shown — never jokey, never silly.
- **Crafted** — Reads as considered, like the work. No filler, no bluster.

**Voice qualities**
- **Say it straight.** *Do*: "Here's the timeline and the price. No surprises." *Don't*: "We leverage a holistic, synergistic engagement model."
- **Show, don't claim.** *Do*: "See the site we shipped for X — 0.9s load, built in six weeks." *Don't*: "We're a world-class, award-winning, results-driven agency."
- **Confident, not loud.** *Do*: "We design it and we build it. Same team." *Don't*: "We're revolutionising the entire industry!!!"
- **Witty, not silly.** *Do*: "Your brand should look as good as your idea. Usually it doesn't. We fix that." *Don't*: pun-filled, meme-heavy, or emoji-stacked copy.
- **Specific over vague.** *Do*: "Custom-coded, not a page builder." *Don't*: "Cutting-edge digital solutions."

### Writing Style

- **Sentence length**: short to medium. Lead with the point. Fragments are allowed for emphasis.
- **Punctuation**: em-dashes for asides; minimal exclamation marks (one is plenty, rarely); the en-dash/em-dash house style used throughout the brand. Oxford comma optional but be consistent per piece.
- **Capitalization**: sentence case for headlines and UI (not Title Case, not ALL CAPS — except short mono labels, which may be uppercase). "Aditeraction" always one word, capital A.
- **Numbers**: spell out one to nine in prose; use numerals for 10+, all metrics, prices, specs and dates (e.g. "six weeks", "0.9s", "10 projects", "₹/$").
- **Contractions**: use them — "we're", "don't", "it's". They keep the voice casual and human.

### Vocabulary

**We say**: design and engineering under one roof · idea to launch · we ship · founder-led · end to end · custom-coded · craft you can feel · one partner · say it straight · premium, not template · on time, in your time zone · the work speaks

**We don't say**: synergy · leverage · world-class · revolutionary · cutting-edge · best-in-class · one-stop shop · solutions provider · circle back · disrupt · unlock value · ninja/rockstar/guru · "as an agency, we…" · unquantified superlatives

## PART 4 — MESSAGING

### Core Message

**Design and engineering under one roof — we take founders from idea to launched brand, site and app.**

### Value Proposition

Aditeraction is the one partner that both designs your brand and writes the production code that ships it — so nothing gets lost between a nice mockup and a live product. You get agency-level craft without agency overheads, a founder you talk to directly, and work that launches on time across Indian and international time zones.

### Tagline

Two **LOCKED** lines. **Rule:** never put both lines on the same visual line — the tagline leads, the descriptor supports (below it, or in a separate context).

**Primary tagline — "Idea to launched."**
The headline promise. Use it under the logo, in the website hero headline, social bios and billboards. The full stop is always **Vermilion #FF4D2E** wherever colour is available. Use once per surface; never stretch it into a sentence or add punctuation beyond the approved form.

**Descriptor line — "Brand, site, app. One roof."**
Explains what we do. Use it as the website sub-headline, and in pitch decks, proposals, LinkedIn and the email signature.

**Alternates — not in use:**
- **"Designed and shipped."** — States the dual discipline (design + engineering) as a single outcome.
- **"From sketch to shipped."** — Concrete and craft-forward; mirrors the mission line.
- **"One team. Idea to launch."** — Leads with the end-to-end, one-partner differentiator.

### Key Messages

- **One partner, idea to launch.** Brand, site, app, marketing and video in one plan, one timeline, one point of contact. *Proof*: single scope/timeline/contact across the full project; six end-to-end case studies on the site.
- **Design and code are one discipline.** The same team designs the brand and writes the production code, so nothing is lost in handoff. *Proof*: in-house design + engineering; custom-coded sites and apps (incl. Shopify), not templates or page builders.
- **Global-grade craft, lean-studio speed and cost.** Agency-level quality without agency overheads, working across India, US, UK, EU and Middle East time zones. *Proof*: founder-led delivery; shipped work with real performance metrics; timezone-aligned communication.
- **Built to perform, not just to look good.** Fast, custom-coded experiences where the site itself is the proof of craft. *Proof*: load times and build timelines quoted per case study; "show, don't claim".

## PART 5 — BRAND IN USE

### Digital

**Website** — Dark by default (Ink canvas, Paper text). Header: minimal, logo lockup left, mono nav labels, one Vermilion accent (active/hover). Hero: cinematic 3D/WebGL stage built from the mark's geometry with the logo animating in — never a gradient wallpaper. CTAs: solid Vermilion button with Paper label, or Paper outline on Ink; one primary CTA per view. Section numbers and specs in mono. The six case studies are the spine; the site's own performance and motion are the pitch.

**Social media** — Profiles: icon-only mark on Ink as the avatar; consistent dark templates with mono labels and `01 —` numbering for instant recognisability. **LinkedIn**: case-study breakdowns and founder POV — straight-talking, confident, specific. **Instagram**: work, motion clips, process crops. One accent per post. Captions in brand voice: lead with the point, show the work, no hashtag soup.

**Email** — Header: wordmark on Ink or Paper. Signature format:
```
Aditya — Founder, Aditeraction
Brand, site, app. One roof.
info@aditeraction.com · aditeraction.com
```
Mono for the contact line; one Vermilion accent link. Tone: short, direct, human; contractions on.

### Print

**Business cards** — Paper off-white stock with Ink type and a single Vermilion detail (or Ink card, Paper type, reversed mark). Hierarchy: name + role → contact (mono) → wordmark/symbol. Symbol printed solid or debossed; legible at small size.

**Letterhead** — Paper surface, Ink type, wordmark top-left, mono footer with contact + site. Digital equivalent: the same layout as an A4/Letter PDF template and the invoice template (mono for figures and line items — honest, legible, premium).

**Presentations** — Ink background default; Paper for dense/readable pages. Display grotesque headlines kept short; mono for data, scope, timelines and pricing. One full-bleed Vermilion statement slide reserved for the single key moment (the offer / the idea). Consistent `01 —` numbering ties decks to web. Min body size 18px-equivalent; one accent per slide.

### Brand Don'ts

- [ ] Never use the icon-only logo below **16px**, or the full lockup below **24px / 18mm** (switch to icon-only).
- [ ] Never add shadows, glows, bevels, gradients or 3D effects to the logo itself.
- [ ] Never use colours outside the approved palette, or Vermilion for small body text.
- [ ] Never use more than **one** accent moment per screen/slide.
- [ ] Never use fonts outside the brand system (grotesque + mono only; no serifs/scripts).
- [ ] Never write in passive, corporate, jargon-heavy tone — no "synergy", "world-class", "revolutionary".
- [ ] Never claim without proof — show shipped work and real numbers.
- [ ] Never use agency-stock clichés or AI-gradient imagery.
- [ ] Never break WCAG AA contrast for text (≥4.5:1 body).
- [ ] Never reference, reuse or copy any previous company's assets, lines or claims.
